# E-commerce Sales Analytics | SQL & Google Sheets

## Project Overview

This is a self-initiated E-commerce Sales Analytics project focused on transforming raw sales data into meaningful business insights using MySQL and Google Sheets.

The project explores sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product categories, and geographic trends.

Project Type: Independent Portfolio Project  
Dataset: Synthetic E-commerce Sales Dataset (2021–2024)  
Database: MySQL  
Dashboard Tool:Google Sheets

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Track important business KPIs.
- Identify monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Compare product category performance.
- Understand customer segment contribution.
- Analyze geographic sales performance.
- Explore the relationship between discounts and profitability.

## Business KPIs

The dashboard includes:

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Profit Margin
- Average Order Value

## Dashboard Analysis

The Google Sheets dashboard explores:

- Monthly Revenue Trends
- Monthly Profit Trends
- Revenue by Product Category
- Profit by Product Category
- Customer Segment Performance
- Top Products by Revenue
- Revenue by Region
- Discount vs. Non-Discount Performance

## Key Business Insight

The discount analysis explores an important business question:

**Are discounts increasing revenue while maintaining profitability?**

The analysis compares revenue, profit, and profit margins between discounted and non-discounted orders.

This helps demonstrate why revenue growth should also be evaluated alongside profitability.
